    Emergency Guidebook

    Thunderstorm

    Before Thunderstorm

    1. Check PAGASA advisories for local thunderstorm warnings.
    2. Stock food, clean water, medicines, and flashlights.
    3. Prepare an emergency kit and important documents.
    4. Stay updated with weather alerts and evacuation notices.

    During Thunderstorm

    1. Stay indoors and away from windows.
    2. Unplug electronics to protect from sudden lightning power surges.
    3. Avoid plumbing and running waters to avoid potential electrical charges.
    4. Move to evacuation centers if instructed by authorities.

    After Thunderstorm

    1. Stay inside for at least 30 minutes after the thunderstorm.
    2. Avoid fallen and exposed cables and report broken utility lines.
    3. Inspect your home for any damages.
    4. Check appliances and electronics before using them again.

    Flood

    Before Flood

    1. Move valuables and important documents to higher places.
    2. Prepare emergency kit (food, water, flashlight, first aid).
    3. Stay updated with weather alerts and warnings.
    4. Park your car on higher ground to avoid water damage.

    During Flood

    1. Go to higher ground immediately.
    2. Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters.
    3. If trapped in a car, abandon it if water rises and it's safe to exit.
    4. Keep communication devices charged and listen to official instructions.

    After Flood

    1. Return home only when authorities say it's safe.
    2. Do not start a flooded car until it's checked for damage.
    3. Watch out for damaged roads, power lines, and contaminated water.
    4. Help neighbors if possible and report hazards.

    Emergency Guidebook

    Typhoon

    Before Typhoon

    1. Secure your home (windows, doors, roof).
    2. Stock food, clean water, medicines, and flashlights.
    3. Prepare an emergency kit and important documents.
    4. Stay updated with weather alerts and evacuation notices.

    During Typhoon

    1. Stay indoors and away from windows.
    2. Avoid floodwaters and keep safe from flying debris.
    3. Keep communication devices charged and listen to official advisories.
    4. Move to evacuation centers if instructed by authorities.

    After Typhoon

    1. Check for hazards like downed power lines or damaged structures.
    2. Assist neighbors if safe and report damages.
    3. Follow local recovery instructions and wait for clearance before returning home.
    4. Clean and disinfect water sources to avoid contamination.

    Earthquake

    Before Earthquake

    1. Secure heavy furniture, appliances, and overhead fixtures to walls.
    2. Prepare an emergency supply kit with essential medications and water.
    3. Identify safe spots in each room (under sturdy tables, internal walls).
    4. Plan and practice family earthquake response and evacuation routes.

    During Earthquake

    1. Drop, Cover, and Hold On under a sturdy table or desk.
    2. Stay away from glass, windows, outside doors, and exterior walls.
    3. If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, and power lines.
    4. If in a moving vehicle, pull over safely and stay inside until shaking stops.

    After Earthquake

    1. Be prepared for aftershocks and evacuate safely if structure is damaged.
    2. Check yourself and others for injuries; apply first aid if trained.
    3. Inspect gas, water, and electrical lines for damage or leaks.
    4. Listen to local emergency broadcasts for public safety instructions.

    First Aid

    First Aid Guide

    • Bleeding — apply firm, direct pressure with a clean cloth and elevate the wound.
    • Burns — cool under running water for 10–20 minutes; don't apply ice.
    • Choking — 5 back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts, repeat.
    • Fainting — lay the person flat, raise their legs, loosen tight clothing.
